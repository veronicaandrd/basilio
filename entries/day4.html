Something resolved differently overnight than I expected from how the evening closed; the roots that had quieted woke drawing again, the soil already thinned by morning. The light arrived briefly, less than it has been. Water came in the afternoon and I took it, but by now it is already moving through, the evening reading lower than I would have guessed. Whatever steadiness yesterday promised did not carry.
