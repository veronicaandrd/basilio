The dryness had not shifted by morning; the soil held it through the day. The bright returned in separate arrivals, split in a way I had not felt before. Water came before dark but this time gave almost nothing; I could barely register the change. I am at the edge of something, the soil tight and unresponsive in a way I do not have a word for.
