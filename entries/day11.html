Basilio
Day 11

The light gave more today than it has since I arrived, and returned to the morning where it belongs; the soil had little to offer in return. Something in the outside air is heavy with moisture I cannot reach. The light's abundance and the soil's want pressed against each other all day, and I do not know what to make of a generous day that I could only partly use.
