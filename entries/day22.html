What the light is doing now registers differently from what it has given before; the duration has reached a kind of ceiling, and the approach to that ceiling carries its own quality. The morning brought the direct bright back, and the soil was adequate to meet it, briefly. By evening the draw returned it to dry. The night outside is colder than any I have known.
