The soil has drawn past the edge I have been approaching; I am somewhere I have not been before. The light gave well and I could barely use it, the bright long and present while the roots had too little. Something in the draw tonight pulls upward with a force I cannot account for, as if the dark above has weight. The soil is not ready for the night ahead.
