Day 29

The watering gave well at midday and by evening the soil had almost nothing left; the heat between took it all. I have not been this warm before, and the warmth does not carry the same quality as the milder days; it presses differently, sits in the air rather than passing through. The soil is dry again and the warmth is still here at this hour, which is new.
