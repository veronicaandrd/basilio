Day 28

What the air carries today is almost entirely warmth; the moisture has gone from it. The morning gave generous light and the soil had enough to meet it; what the two produced together felt different from ordinary adequacy. The light is close to its peak and I register that approach in the way the body registers something arriving. By evening the soil holds, the dry warmth pulling at it.
