Day 5

The morning brought something less than what I have come to half-expect; no force to it, just a mild brightness that did not open much. The soil has been drawing all day and nothing came to answer it; the long presence withdrew as before and the water did not follow. I am sitting with a dryness that is becoming familiar in a way I am not sure is comfortable.
