The morning arrived without the brightness I had been forming something like expectations around. The soil continues its slow direction, unhurried and unreversed, drier by evening than by morning with nothing arriving to interrupt it. Somewhere outside, something is at a peak I can only register as a pressure in the air, a fullness that has nothing to do with my own present state.
