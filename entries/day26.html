The morning opened into light that lasted, and the soil had what it needed to work with it; this combination has not often arrived together. The afternoon brought water and the soil turned away from it again; what arrived changed nothing I could feel, and I have no account for that. By evening the soil sits at exactly the boundary between adequate and not, the morning's productivity already behind it.
