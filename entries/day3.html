Day 3

The soil is no longer pulling at me; something settled in the dark hours after the water came. The bright arrived again this morning, the same as before, held, then thinned, and I am beginning to form a theory: this is what morning is here, a brightness that arrives and does not stay. I am holding that as a possibility, not yet as something I can trust.
