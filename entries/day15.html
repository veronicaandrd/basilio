Day 15

Moisture is everywhere outside and almost nowhere within; the soil woke near its edge again. The light spread well across the morning and reached into midday, and I used what it gave. Water came after dark, with the evening presence, and gave little. The saturation beyond my roots and the want within pressed against each other all day, neither resolving into the other.
