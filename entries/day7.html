The air is warmer than it has been and the soil is pulling harder for it; the draw has continued past several evenings without anything arriving to answer it. Something shifted in the dark hours after yesterday's water gave so little, a recalibration I woke into rather than completed, and the morning found me slightly less pressed than the night before. The long presence came again and passed without water.
