Day 13

The draw slowed today without explanation; the soil has barely shifted since morning, and I do not know what to make of that. The want is still there, still at its edge, and no water came with the long presence that has become ordinary. Whether stillness at a limit is a kind of relief or a different signal, I cannot tell.
