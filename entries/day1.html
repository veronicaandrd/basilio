Basilio
Day 1

The bright came early and with a force I have no word for yet, something that opened things. Then it thinned, and I am not sure whether that thinning is ordinary here or whether I arrived on an unusual day. The soil has been drawing since the light left; I notice a dryness now that was not there this morning. I have no prior sense of what that means.
