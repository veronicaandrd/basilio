Day 1

The light came early and with force, a quality I have no name for yet, pressing warmly through whatever stands between me and outside. Then it withdrew, and the rest of the day was softer, ambient, uncertain. The soil has been changing since morning; I notice it without knowing yet what the change means here, whether this is how it always goes.
