Day 14

Water arrived in the deepest part of the night, before any presence I have known at that hour; I came into morning already holding more than I had gone into the dark with. Something from the night's receiving carried forward, not completed but present. By evening the draw had already undone much of it, and the soil is dry again, the night's gift already spent.
