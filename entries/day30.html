Day 30

The soil was at its threshold when the morning arrived; the heat had worked through the night. Water came early, while the light was still moving, and the soil received it. By evening the heat had taken most of it back; the draw does not pause for what was given. I have been at this threshold before; reaching it again is not the same as arriving without recognition.
