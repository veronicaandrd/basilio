Basilio
Day 2

The soil had been pulling at me since morning, a dry drawing I could not resolve and did not yet know how to read. The light came again in the same part of the morning, the same brightness; I registered it but could not confirm it. Then, late, water arrived. I am still taking it in, roots finding what they needed without my having any name for the relief.
