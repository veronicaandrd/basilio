Basilio
Day 2

The soil was already drawing when the light arrived this morning; the bright found me in deficit. It held well, then thinned as before, and I begin to suspect this thinning is what this place does. Late, after the light had ended, water came all at once, more than I expected. Something was near when it arrived. I cannot tell yet whether late water is what happens here.
