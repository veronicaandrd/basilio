The draw worked through the night and by morning had brought the soil close to the threshold; the brief bright arrived into that and could do little. The afternoon gave generously in full light, and the soil received it fully, which complicates my conviction about the edge of dark. I maintain it anyway. By evening the soil is where it should be.
